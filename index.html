About the institute

A focused academic environment in the heart of Prayagraj.

Krishna Coaching Institute is located on Moti Lal Nehru Road, Allen Ganj, directly opposite the University of Allahabad. The institute’s publicly listed programmes cover school-board preparation and competitive entrance categories including NEET, CUET-UG and B.Sc. Nursing.

Teaching approach

Concept-first learning

The academic approach is built around explaining concepts clearly, then reinforcing them through guided problem-solving and revision. The exact teaching methodology should be confirmed with the institute.

Academic support

Guidance and practice

Students may receive classroom teaching, notes and practice support depending on the programme. Availability of specific materials and doubt-clearing sessions should be confirmed for each batch.
